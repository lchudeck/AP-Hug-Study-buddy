## Adult reviewer prompts

Ask reviewers to address whichever they actually tested:

- Was it easy to understand where to begin?
- Did question explanations help after a mistake?
- Did the language feel appropriate for ninth graders?
- Did the unit organization match APHG instruction?
- Was the FRQ guidance accurate and useful?
- Did the mobile experience work?
- Did the no-login privacy model influence the decision to use it?

Do not script the review, offer compensation for positive language, or suppress criticism.

## Anonymous classroom pilot

Collect only aggregate, non-identifying results:

- Number of students invited
- Number who voluntarily used the tool
- Unit/topics practiced
- Anonymous pre/post question accuracy by class aggregate
- Completion rate by anonymous aggregate
- Short optional feedback with identifying details removed

Do not publish individual student scores, names, class periods, school IDs, or quotations that identify a minor.
