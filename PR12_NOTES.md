# PR 12 — Final Freshman Polish

Scope is intentionally narrow.

- Keep every existing study tool and academic feature.
- Make the most important freshman paths visually dominant in the top navigation.
- De-emphasize advanced/secondary tabs without hiding them.
- Add a brief positive progress payoff after a real result is shown.
- Give students three clear next steps: practice a weak spot, review the unit, or finish for now.
- Do not change scoring, question selection, mastery thresholds, FRQ grading, or student data storage.
