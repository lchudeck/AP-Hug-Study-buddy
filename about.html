About APHUG Study Buddy

A focused learning tool for students taking AP Human Geography—often as ninth graders in their first AP course.

Why it exists

APHUG students need more than a pile of definitions. They need a clear path from learning a concept to recognizing it in a scenario, reading geographic evidence, explaining relationships, and writing concise FRQ responses.

Study Buddy was created by a classroom AP Human Geography teacher to make those steps visible and manageable.

What makes it different

  • Designed specifically for AP Human Geography rather than every school subject
  • Written for ninth-grade accessibility without lowering the AP-level target
  • Organized around all seven CED units and their topic structure
  • Adaptive topic and misconception follow-up
  • Point-by-point FRQ coaching
  • Map, data, image, and source-based practice
  • No required student identity account

Editorial principles

Content should be accurate, readable, aligned to current course expectations, honest about the limits of automated feedback, and revised when student testing exposes confusion. Study Buddy does not present its built-in feedback as official AP scoring.

Independent status

APHUG Study Buddy is created independently by C&C Digital Ventures. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by the College Board.

For reviewers and teachers: the application can be used without creating a student account. Please evaluate the live question explanations, unit coverage, FRQ guidance, mobile experience, and privacy model directly.
