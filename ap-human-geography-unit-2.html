High-priority distinctions

  • Arithmetic, physiological, and agricultural density measure different relationships between population, total land, arable land, and farmers.
  • DTM stage vs. population-pyramid shape: related evidence, but not interchangeable terms.
  • NIR vs. total population change: natural increase excludes migration.
  • Push/pull factors vs. intervening opportunities or obstacles: reasons for movement versus conditions altering the route or destination.
  • Refugee, asylum seeker, and IDP: legal and spatial categories that should not be treated as synonyms.

Apply it

Use demographic evidence to explain a consequence: school demand, labor supply, pensions, health care, housing, political representation, resource pressure, or migration policy.
