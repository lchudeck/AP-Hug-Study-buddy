Cultural Patterns and Processes

Explain how culture shapes landscapes and how language, religion, identity, and cultural practices spread and change.

Core ideas

High-priority distinctions

  • Relocation diffusion: people move and carry a cultural trait.
  • Contagious diffusion: rapid person-to-person spread across space.
  • Hierarchical diffusion: spread through influential people, institutions, or major places.
  • Stimulus diffusion: the underlying idea spreads but is adapted.
  • Assimilation, acculturation, and syncretism: different degrees and forms of cultural change.

Apply it

Name the diffusion process only after identifying the mechanism. A strong explanation links transportation, migration, media, trade, power, or communication networks to the observed cultural pattern.
