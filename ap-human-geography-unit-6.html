AP Human Geography Unit 6

Cities and Urban Land Use

Explain how cities grow, how land uses are organized, and how urban change creates benefits, costs, and unequal outcomes.

Practice Unit 6

Core ideas

UrbanizationUrban HierarchyCity ModelsSuburbanizationInfrastructureSustainabilityGentrification

High-priority distinctions

  • Site vs. situation of cities: local physical setting versus connections and relative location.
  • CBD vs. edge city: traditional commercial core versus suburban concentration of jobs and retail.
  • Concentric, sector, and multiple-nuclei models: different simplified explanations of urban land use.
  • Urban renewal vs. gentrification: redevelopment processes are related, but gentrification emphasizes neighborhood change and possible displacement.
  • Benefit vs. cost: investment and services may improve while affordability and belonging decline for existing residents.

Apply it

Urban questions reward attention to scale. A citywide average can hide neighborhood inequality. Name the process, use the evidence, and explain who benefits, who bears costs, and why.

Unit 6 checkpoint: Can you evaluate one urban policy using both a benefit and a possible limitation?
