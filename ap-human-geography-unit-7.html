AP Human Geography Unit 7

Industrial and Economic Development

Measure development, explain industrial location, and evaluate how trade and globalization affect places differently.

Practice Unit 7

Core ideas

Development MeasuresEconomic SectorsIndustrial LocationRostowWorld-SystemsTradeGlobalizationSustainable Development

High-priority distinctions

  • GDP/GNI per capita vs. HDI: income measures versus a broader composite including health and education.
  • Primary, secondary, tertiary, quaternary, and quinary sectors: different types of economic activity.
  • Bulk-reducing vs. bulk-gaining industries: location near inputs versus markets.
  • Rostow vs. world-systems theory: staged modernization versus relationships among core, semiperiphery, and periphery.
  • Outsourcing vs. offshoring: contracting work externally versus relocating activity abroad.

Apply it

Do not describe “development” with one number. Select an appropriate indicator, explain what it reveals, identify what it misses, and connect global economic changes to uneven local outcomes.
