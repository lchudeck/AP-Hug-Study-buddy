Why explanations matter

A practice test is useful only if the student learns from the misses. Study Buddy states the correct answer, explains the geographic idea, records evidence by topic, and deliberately returns to recurring confusions.

Mastery is more than one good score

The mastery system looks for repeated evidence: multiple attempts, strong accuracy, a current correct streak, and successful work on more than one study day. Progress is stored locally on the device, not in a student identity profile.

No account required. Students can practice without providing a real name, email address, birthday, school, school ID, or location.
