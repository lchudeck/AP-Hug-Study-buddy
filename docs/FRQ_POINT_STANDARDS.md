# Point-level practice standards

Original Study Buddy criteria, not official College Board scoring guidelines. Human teacher sign-off remains pending. Concise answers earn credit when they supply the complete requirement; spelling/grammar are not separate criteria. A partial answer omits a required detail or relationship. Unrecognized plausible wording requires review.

## Unit 3

| Point / concept and task | Minimum criterion / alternatives | Complete example | Partial / insufficient | Misconception / contradiction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A · Identify: Identify the term for the place where a cultural trait begins. | Name the geographic term for a cultural trait’s place of origin. Alternative: A cultural hearth | The hearth. | The beginning. / Where culture starts. | Relocation diffusion. / Stimulus diffusion |
| B · Define: Define relocation diffusion. | Define how movement of people carries a cultural trait to a new place. | C · Describe: Describe one example of stimulus diffusion. | Give a specific example of a spreading idea being adapted to local culture. | D · Explain: Explain how migration can spread culture. | Explain how migrating people carry cultural traits to new places. | G · Explain: Explain one effect of cultural diffusion on local cultures. | Explain a specific change to local culture and connect it to incoming cultural influences.
