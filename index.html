AP Human Geography practice built for learning and exam preparation

APHUG Study Buddy is a free, teacher-built AP Human Geography study and exam-practice tool. Students can review every CED unit, practice vocabulary and geographic models, work through maps, data, and stimulus-based questions, and use point-by-point FRQ coaching. Adaptive practice returns students to weak topics and common misconceptions instead of treating one correct answer as permanent mastery.
