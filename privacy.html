Privacy & Classroom Use

A plain-language explanation for students, families, and schools.

What Study Buddy collects

Study Buddy does not ask for or collect a student's name, email address, birthday, school ID, location, or account password. There are no student accounts, advertisements, or payments in the classroom version.

How progress works

Practice progress and written drafts are stored in the web browser's local storage on the device being used. The information stays in that browser and is not sent to a teacher dashboard. Students should use the same assigned device when possible. Clearing browser data can remove saved progress.

Classroom expectations

  • Use Study Buddy for learning and practice; a Study Buddy score is not a class grade or an official AP score.
  • Do not enter names, email addresses, school IDs, or other personal information in written-answer or problem-report boxes.
  • Check explanations and course materials when an answer seems wrong, and use the anonymous problem-report button to flag it.
  • Follow your teacher's directions and your school's acceptable-use rules.
