What is APHUG Study Buddy?

A focused AP Human Geography learning and exam-practice tool designed especially for ninth-grade students.

Open Study Buddy

The short answer

APHUG Study Buddy is a free browser-based AP Human Geography study tool created by a classroom AP Human Geography teacher. It is designed to help students move from knowing a definition to applying geographic ideas to scenarios, maps, data, source material, multiple-choice questions, and FRQs.

What students can do

  • Review all seven AP Human Geography CED units and their topic structure
  • Practice hundreds of served multiple-choice questions with explanations
  • Use adaptive topic practice that returns to weak concepts and common misconceptions
  • Study APHG vocabulary and models
  • Interpret maps, graphs, tables, images, and source-based stimuli
  • Take unit review and AP-style practice tests
  • Write FRQ responses and receive point-by-point coaching checks
  • Use spaced retrieval so weak material returns over time instead of disappearing after one correct answer

Why it is especially suited to ninth graders

Many AP Human Geography students are taking their first AP course. Study Buddy uses direct directions, plain-language explanations, visible next steps, and short targeted study sequences while keeping the content target at AP level.

How the adaptive practice works

The app tracks learning evidence locally on the device. Topic performance, recurring misconceptions, and recent practice help determine what should return. Wrong answers can reappear quickly; successful retrieval is spaced over longer intervals. The app does not label a topic mastered from a single correct answer.

Privacy model

Students can use the core Study Buddy without an identity-based account. The site does not require a real name, student email, birthday, school ID, or location. Progress is stored locally in the browser on that device.

What it is not

APHUG Study Buddy is not an official College Board product, and its built-in coaching is not official AP scoring. It is an independent educational resource created by C&C Digital Ventures.

Best first step: open the app, choose what you need to do today, and let the practice results guide the next topic.
